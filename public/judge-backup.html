06 · FIX OPTIMIZER

Break dangerous routes
with the least disruption.

Objective: minimum cost to block the modeled Critical and High routes. Costs are illustrative disruption points.

Invalidate exposed credential access 1 · blocks A
Restrict public staging access 2 · blocks B
Patch and verify vulnerable service 4 · blocks B
Revoke and replace vendor credential 2 · blocks C
Recommended: credential invalidation + public restriction = 3 points. A and B blocked; C remains.

Exhaustive search over four predefined actions. Selection and simulation are separate; no production system changes.
